What was wrong

Outflow split: blood was divided between the two outlets with the wrong ratio (160 cases).

Rough wall: scan noise left bumps on the vessel surface that distort shear stress (64 cases). These walls were smoothed first.

Since the last update

500+ new synthetic aneurysms

Our generative model, AneuG, draws aneurysm shapes that do not come from any patient. Version 2 builds each one in two learned steps, shown below on generated shapes.

Also new: when real aneurysms are fitted for training, the template's openings no longer have to land exactly on the patient's openings.

Aneurysm dome Inlet Outlet Second outlet

It labels the dome and every opening, then cuts the openings so vessels can be attached.

The centerline is drawn one point at a time. Each new point looks back at every point before it, so the branches come out longer and more natural, ready to be turned into tubes and merged with the dome.
